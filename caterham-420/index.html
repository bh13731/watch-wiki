Caterham 420R race-car market (for-sale cars, two specs)

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Tracker for Caterham Seven 420R race cars to buy, in both specs with a UK grid: OFFICIAL (Caterham Motorsport's Seven Championship UK, with BARC) and 7 RACING (https://seven.racing, run by McMillan Motorsport). Fed by the daily caterham-420-watch cron (06:40 London); dedup lines live in _ledger.md with slug prefix c420-; full catalog in caterham-420-watch/listings.md; price bands, rule differences, budgets and sold comps in caterham-420-watch/comps.md. Rules summary for the official series: [seven-uk-2026-championship.md](seven-uk-2026-championship.md).

Current state (2026-11-19): 13 race cars live (10 UK, 3 Iberia); every price is an ask. OFFICIAL-spec cars ask £38,000–£44,995 (4 live; last sold asks £32,000–£36,000). 7 RACING-spec 2018–19 cars ask £25,500–£29,995 (4 live incl. one claiming both; sold asks £22,950–£28,995). No R300 race car is live. New 420R Race Package from £53,990 kit including a season of entries. Strongest OFFICIAL car: Williams 2019 at £38,000 (July 2026 engine, SWR gearbox refresh, 3rd in 2025). Strongest 7 RACING car: Alpha 7 2019 at £27,995 (zero-hour engine with dyno sheets). Over-asks: DPR #34 2022 at £44,995 (stored since 2023, long front) and PT's 2012-chassis car at £28,995.

The two specs are different cars. OFFICIAL is the current post-2020 420R: Caterham-built sealed engine on the latest Caterham tune, gearbox sealed by Caterham or SWR, 350 lb springs with re-valved dampers, Nova slicks (£1,017.60 a set inc VAT, one new set per weekend). 7 RACING freezes the car at pre-2020 spec and its Reg 5.2.2 bars the post-2020 car as it stands: Caterham or McMillan engine on a McMillan pre-2020 tune, gearbox and diff the competitor may rebuild, 250 lb springs on original dampers, treaded MRF ZTI2 tyres (£561.60 a set inc VAT) for wet and dry. Both: 635 kg with driver, SADEV 6-speed, Titan LSD, no road-car conversions, R300 updates allowed. In dry 2026 running the OFFICIAL car was 4.1–4.7 s a lap faster in races at Donington GP, Snetterton 300 and Brands GP.

Switching. OFFICIAL to 7 RACING is the cheap direction (tyres, springs, damper valving, tune re-flash, rear camera; sealed Caterham engine and gearbox accepted). 7 RACING to OFFICIAL is the risky one: Caterham must accept and seal engine, gearbox and diff, and a McMillan-built engine does not qualify. Only tyre prices are published. So an OFFICIAL car keeps both doors open; a 7 RACING car mostly does not.

Series. Seven UK 2026 grids 13–23 (about 19 typical); 7 weekends of 3 standing-start races; Race National licence plus 12 races; registration £775 (2026). 2027 provisional: Snetterton 24–25 Apr, Anglesey 15–16 May, Croft 12–13 Jun, Brands 3–4 Jul, Zandvoort 24–25 Jul, Donington 18–19 Sep, Silverstone 23–24 Oct. 7 Racing 420R class 2026 grids 12–16 (mid-thirties were reported in 2022); 6 events, 14 rolling-start races, reverse top ten in the last race; Interclub licence; approved teams only; membership £595 + VAT until 31 Dec 2026 then £695 + VAT, six 2027 UK entries £3,760 + VAT. 2027 UK: Donington GP 3–4 Apr, Silverstone GP 8–9 May, Croft 26 Jun, Brands GP 10 Jul, Snetterton 300 4–5 Sep, Castle Combe 2 Oct; plus a 420R-only European series (Algarve, Barcelona, Jarama, Valencia, Silverstone GP, Estoril; provisional, no prices). 7 Racing is run by McMillan Motorsport, which is also its engine builder, tune and tyre supplier and a competitor. It dates from 2012 (7 Race Series with MSVR from 2017, renamed 2024).

Season budgets (inc VAT, self-run to fully supported; mostly estimates): Seven UK £30,700–£69,800; 7 Racing UK £22,500–£53,200; 7 Racing Europe £38,400–£74,200. No price is published anywhere for an engine rebuild, SADEV rebuild, team support or an arrive-and-drive 420R seat.

Standing observations: RCD carries almost the whole market and re-dates adverts on renewal (the advert number shows true age). PistonHeads titles the Williams car '2.0 6-Speed Sequential Petrol', so a '420R' keyword search misses it. No auction has sold a 420R race car. Weak points: driveshafts since the 2021 move to slicks, rear basket and diff mounts, clutch, De Dion ears; a long-front chassis repair is £7.5k–£20k. CSCC Magnificent Sevens accepts 420R race cars; Caterham Graduates does not.
