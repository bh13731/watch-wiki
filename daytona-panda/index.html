Standing observations

  • WoS Group CPO stock is ~90% black-dial 116500LN; white pandas sell within days (3 recent Goldsmiths CPO white sales at £27.95–30.45k).

  • Watchfinder's 'white dial' filter is unreliable — confirm dial from photos before reporting.
