Ferrari F40 market

Last updated: 2026-10-02

Tracker for the daily ferrari-f40-watch (06:40 London). Scope: every F40 for sale in the UK/EU/US plus LM/Competizione anywhere; tiers S (LM/Competizione/GT) → 1 (non-cat/non-adjust) → 2 (cat/adjust) → 3 (US-spec/story). Brief and per-car catalogue live in ferrari-f40-watch/ (BRIEF.md, listings.md); dedup ledger is cars/_ledger.md with f40- slugs. Baselined 2026-09-17. Source notes: AutoTrader/Car & Classic/PistonHeads/dealer pages render via r.jina.ai; AutoScout24/mobile.de/Classic Driver block jina (use search snippets, mark unverified); RM lot pages render; Mecum blocked.

State of the market (baseline 2026-09-17): The F40 has re-rated hard in 2026 — five of six major-auction sales cleared $5m and RM Monterey (Aug) set a standard-car record at $8.365m for the ex-Iacocca US-spec car; the 12-month auction median is ~$3.7m. US-spec cars, once the discount tier, now out-trade European cars at US auctions (Kissimmee $6.6m/$5.83m, Indy $5.17m). Europe is calmer: RM Monaco (Apr) €4.3m for a 1,799-km non-cat Red Book car, RM Woodcote Park (Jul) £3.605m for the Michelotto 'Jean Sage' conversion, and Simon Furlonger sold a UK-supplied non-cat off a £2,499,990 ask. UK retail supply is thin: DK's 1991 cat/non-adjust at £3,894,995 (Red Book pending) and Jeremy Cottingham's 25-year-single-owner 1992 at £3,000,000 (tanks 2021) are the only priced UK cars; Amari's Classiche 1992 is POA; Car & Classic carries several anonymous London POA ads. Two RM lots ahead: Zurich 26 Sep (1991 Swiss Red Book car, CHF3.2–3.5m, spare tanks unfitted) and London 31 Oct (1988 chassis titled 1994, £1.8–2.4m — story pending). No LM/Competizione/GT public offering. Bladders remain the key negotiating lever (~£35k).

Update 2026-10-02: RM London Lot 8 (31 Oct, chassis 78623, titled 1994) finally has its catalogue, and the story is out: it's a Euro 1988 road car that spent its life in Japan and was cosmetically converted there to look like an F40 LM — standard road suspension underneath, air jacks and non-standard brakes/coolers added, non-original fuel delivery/intercooler/wastegate, and the original odometer is missing (the replacement reads 30,398 km, unwarranted). Matching-numbers engine and gearbox survive, and RM openly pitches it as a restore-to-stock candidate. The £1.8–2.4m estimate now reads as parts-value-plus: stock early non-cat cars trade £2.5m+, so the spread is the restoration bill and the odometer discount. Rest of market unchanged (AT trio DK £3,894,995 / Cottingham £3.0m / Amari POA; DK Geneva show car POA; Sotheby's US car $2.6m; Mecum Kissimmee Lot R871 no estimate yet; classic.com 1 for sale).

Context: Kissimmee is where the 2026 re-rate started ($6.6m/$5.83m in Jan 2026) — but those were US-spec cars; a 20k-km Euro cat car should trade nearer $3–4m on current comps unless the catalogue adds provenance.
